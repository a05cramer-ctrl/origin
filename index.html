The rules

  1. Every hour at :00 UTC the buyer reads the board.
  2. It takes the runner with the most volume in the last hour and finds its original.
  3. It buys the original with the creator fees collected that hour. If the original has no pool left to buy from, the SOL rolls into the next hour.
  4. Each buy is a Jupiter swap, quoted first and skipped above 4% price impact.
  5. Bought coins are held in the public wallet. Every buy is listed below with its transaction.

Find the family

Take a runner's ticker. Pull every Solana coin that uses it from Jupiter's token index and pump.fun's coin list. Keep the ones with the same name, too: $MEGA "Make Europe Great Again" is family, $MEGA "Mega Dog" is just squatting the ticker.

02

Sort by birth

Each coin's create time is its first pool on-chain, or pump.fun's create timestamp, whichever is earlier. The oldest is the original. Every other coin gets its place: copy #18, and how late it was.

03

Check the picture

Your browser hashes every PFP down to an 8×8 fingerprint and compares it with the original's. A copy that stole the art shows its match score.
